kuboferma-publisher

A private publishing tool for the «Кубоферма» YouTube channel.

kuboferma-publisher is a small self-hosted service used only by the owner of the «Кубоферма» channel. It sends newly produced short animated videos to the owner for review in Telegram and, after the owner approves a video, uploads it to the owner's own YouTube channel with a scheduled publish time.

What it does with YouTube

  • Uploads videos approved by the channel owner to the owner's channel (YouTube Data API videos.insert).
  • Sets or changes the visibility and scheduled publish time of those videos (videos.update).
  • Reads the status and public statistics (views, likes, comments) of the channel's own videos.

The app is not offered to other users. It acts only on the channel of the Google account that authorized it.
