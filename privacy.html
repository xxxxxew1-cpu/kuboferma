Privacy Policy

kuboferma-publisher · Effective date: October 13, 2026

kuboferma-publisher ("the app") is a private, self-hosted tool used solely by the owner of the «Кубоферма» YouTube channel to upload and schedule videos on that channel. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data the app accesses

  • Google OAuth tokens for the channel owner's Google account, granted with the youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl scopes.
  • The channel's own videos: their metadata (title, description, tags), visibility, scheduled publish time, processing status and public statistics (view, like and comment counts).

The app does not access viewers' data, comments' authors, subscribers' data, or any data of other Google users.

How the data is used

  • To upload videos approved by the channel owner and set their visibility and publish time.
  • To check that a video was scheduled or published as requested and to notify the owner.
  • To show the owner a short summary of their own videos' statistics.

Storage and sharing

  • OAuth tokens and the app's working state are stored only on the owner's private server, in files readable only by the service account. They are never logged.
  • No data is sold, shared with, or transferred to third parties. Notifications are sent only to the owner's own Telegram chat.
  • The app has no other users and collects no analytics or cookies.

Retention and revoking access

Tokens are kept until the owner revokes access. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the stored token becomes useless immediately and can be deleted from the server.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
